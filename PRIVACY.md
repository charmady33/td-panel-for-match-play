# TD Panel for Match Play - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

TD Panel for Match Play is a Chrome extension for tournament directors who use Match Play Events
(app.matchplay.events). It is not made by or affiliated with Match Play Events.

## What the extension stores
- **Your Match Play API token**, which you paste in yourself. It is stored only in this browser (Chrome's extension
  storage) and is sent only to Match Play's official API (api.matchplay.events) to read your tournaments.
- **Tournament settings you make in the extension** (cutoffs, IFPA rank cuts, check-in, tiebreaker results, bank and
  round settings) and a short-lived copy of tournament data so pages draw quickly. These are stored only in this browser.

## What the extension sends, and where
- To **app.matchplay.events** and **api.matchplay.events**: reads of your tournaments, and the changes you confirm, made
  by clicking Match Play's own buttons on its own pages.
- To **api.github.com**: a check for a newer version (only when installed outside the Chrome Web Store).
- Nothing else. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.

## Removing your data
Remove your token with the extension's toolbar button, use "Clear saved data for this tournament", or uninstall the
extension (Chrome deletes everything it stored).
